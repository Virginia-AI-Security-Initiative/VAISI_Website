# Drafting Checklist

## Before drafting

- Identify the distribution context: VAISI announcement list, VAISI members,
  or an unrelated external list. Do not use external outreach drafts to infer
  the normal announcement-list voice or audience assumptions.
- Identify the message type: announcement, same-day reminder, roundup, or
  follow-up.
- Identify the audience and desired action.
- Find one or two closest examples in `manifest.json`.
- Pull current title, calendar date, day of week, time, timezone, location,
  description, food/incentive, deadline, and URL from current sources.
- Resolve any conflicts between the user's facts, site, calendar, and form.
- Confirm whether the sender is Seth personally or a VAISI team and verify any
  current title.

## Draft quality

- Main news appears in the first paragraph.
- Date, time, timezone (for virtual or mixed audiences), and location/link are
  unambiguous.
- The action link is present and has clear anchor text.
- The tone is warm, direct, and concise.
- Background requirements and attendance flexibility are mentioned only when
  relevant.
- Food, games, prizes, or other incentives are stated accurately.
- No old logistics were copied from the style samples.
- No transcription artifacts or accidental duplicated phrases remain.
- Signature matches the actual sender.
- For new VAISI announcement emails, the standard GroupMe, Instagram, website,
  and unsubscribe links are included after the signature, with all four lines
  italicized and set to 10-point font.

## Final verification

- Check that the named weekday matches the calendar date.
- Open or otherwise verify every link if tools and access are available.
- Re-read numbers: dates, times, room numbers, dollar values, and deadlines.
- Make the output ready to paste, with no drafting notes inside the email body.
- Keep any assumptions or unresolved questions outside the proposed email.
- Never send without explicit authorization.
