# AI Safety Hackathon: Interpretability, Evaluations, and Security

Hello everyone,

On behalf of one of our faculty advisors, Professor Chirag Agarwal, we wanted to share an upcoming AI Safety Hackathon! This is a great opportunity for students interested in technical ML or AI safety research to get hands-on experience with mechanistic interpretability, safety evaluations, or AI security.

The hackathon will take place over two Fridays at the Capital One Hub:

- **Kickoff:** Friday, October 9 from 9–11 AM
- **Build Day:** Friday, October 16 from 9 AM–5 PM

Participants will work in teams to investigate a question, build a working project, and give a short presentation. No prior interpretability or safety research experience is required, though participants should be comfortable with Python. You can also register individually and be matched with a team.

Registration closes September 25. [Learn more about the hackathon here](https://sig-uva.github.io/) and [register here](https://forms.gle/cPgRN1kDqv9oPWd7A).

Feel free to reach out with any questions!
