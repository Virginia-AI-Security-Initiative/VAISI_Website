# External UVA List Outreach

This directory is deliberately separate from the primary VAISI announcement
list corpus.

## Weighting rule

- Never use drafts in this directory as examples for a normal VAISI
  announcement-list email.
- Even after one of these messages is sent, use it only when drafting for a
  separate academic, departmental, school, course, or UVA-wide interest list.
- For external outreach, use the primary corpus for Seth/VAISI sentence-level
  voice and this corpus for audience framing, self-introduction, and structure.
- Drafts have zero evidentiary weight for personal style until the user confirms
  that they were actually sent. Sent external messages remain secondary style
  evidence because their recipients do not already know VAISI.

## Structural differences from announcement-list emails

External recipients may never have heard of VAISI, so these messages should:

1. Open with a short, audience-specific reason AI safety or governance connects
   to the recipients' field.
2. Introduce VAISI as a UVA community focused on reducing risks from advanced
   AI.
3. Mention concrete programs such as the Intro to AI Safety and AI Policy
   fellowships, events, research, and community.
4. Say that no prior AI safety background is required when accurate.
5. Provide `https://vaisi.org` as a general way to learn more.
6. Give current ways to meet VAISI in person.
7. Omit the VAISI announcement-list explanation and unsubscribe footer.

Avoid claiming that recipients subscribed to hear from VAISI. The department or
list moderator may be forwarding the message on the organization's behalf.

## Excluded channels for this campaign

The source research also named student clubs. At the user's direction, this
campaign excludes club-operated channels, including DSAC, Philosophy Club,
Batten councils, Computer & Network Security Club, WiCS, ColorStack, Google
Developer Student Club, and engineering CIO leader/president lists. Academic
fields can still receive a tailored draft when routed through an official
department, school, program, or course list.
