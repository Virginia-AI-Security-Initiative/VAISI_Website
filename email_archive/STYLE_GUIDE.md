# Seth / VAISI Listserv Style Guide

This guide is descriptive, based on the supplied corpus. Prefer the actual
samples when a pattern and a sample disagree.

## Voice

- Warm, direct, and lightly enthusiastic rather than promotional.
- Write as an organizer speaking to a familiar student community.
- For the announcement list, assume many recipients have engaged before. Invite
  them to `catch up with us` while also welcoming people who are `still looking
  to learn more about what we do`.
- Make participation feel easy: welcome beginners, flexible attendance, and
  questions where relevant.
- Prefer concrete reasons to attend (topic, food, games, prizes, people) over
  broad claims that an event will be exciting.
- Use first-person plural for VAISI announcements (`we`, `our`) and first-person
  singular only when signing personally.

## Typical structure

1. Start with `Hello,`, `Hello everyone,`, or the more casual `Hi everyone!`.
2. Put the main news or reminder in the first paragraph.
3. Give the key action and link early: RSVP, apply, or fill out a short form.
4. Add only the context a reader needs to decide whether to participate.
5. Lower the barrier where useful: no background required, open to all, or
   flexible attendance.
6. Close with a short invitation for questions or anticipation of seeing people.
7. Use a personal or organizational signature appropriate to the sender.
8. Include the listserv explanation/unsubscribe footer on broad event emails
   when applicable.

## Recurring language patterns

- `We are excited to announce ...`
- `We would love to see you there!` / `we'd love to see you there!`
- `Please feel free to join ...`
- `Feel free to reach out with any questions.`
- `Let us know if you have any questions, and looking forward to seeing you all there!`
- `As always, feel free to reach out with any questions.`
- `Thanks,` or `Best,`

Use these selectively. A draft should sound natural, not like a collage of
catchphrases.

## Mechanics

- Use short paragraphs. A simple numbered list works well for a multi-topic
  roundup; labeled lines work well for an event agenda.
- When announcing several nearby opportunities, lead with the number of places
  or ways to find VAISI, then use a numbered list with bold event names. Bold a
  particularly important wayfinding detail when useful (for example, a table
  number and lawn).
- State time and place plainly (`at 6 PM in Monroe 110`).
- Keep link text action-oriented and compact (`RSVP here`, `apply here`,
  `expression of interest form`).
- Exclamation points are welcome but limited, usually one or two per email.
- Parenthetical clarifications are common and useful.
- Contractions are acceptable but not dominant.
- Avoid marketing jargon, elaborate headings, emoji, and excessive formatting.

## Signatures

Personal announcement:

```text
Thanks,
Seth Lifland
President of Virginia AI Security Initiative
```

The title also appears in the shorter form `President of VAISI`. Match the
formality of the message and current office-holder information.

Organizational announcement:

```text
Thanks,
VAISI Officer Team
```

or:

```text
Best,
VAISI Exec
```

Do not assume Seth is the current president; verify the requested sender and
title for every new draft.

## Listserv footer

The supplied event announcements use one of these closely related explanations:

```text
You're on this ListServ because you have recently engaged with our club. Feel free to reach out with any questions.
Click here to unsubscribe: https://lists.virginia.edu/sympa/signoff/vaisi_announcements
```

```text
You've been added to this ListServ because you have recently engaged with our club. Feel free to reach out with any questions.
Click here to unsubscribe: https://lists.virginia.edu/sympa/signoff/vaisi_announcements
```

Use the wording the organization currently prefers. Preserve the unsubscribe
URL exactly unless a newer authoritative source replaces it.

The August 21, 2026 sent email used only this shorter footer, without explaining
why the recipient was on the list:

```text
Click here to unsubscribe: https://lists.virginia.edu/sympa/signoff/vaisi_announcements
```

For similar emails to the existing announcement list, prefer this shorter
footer unless the user asks to restore the explanatory sentence.

## Do not imitate transcription errors

The corpus includes likely rich-text copy/paste corruption, such as repeated
link labels (`apply to one (or bapply...`, `Fill outFill out...`) and encoded
spaces. These are not voice characteristics. Correct typos, broken links, and
formatting in new drafts while retaining the original text in the archive.
